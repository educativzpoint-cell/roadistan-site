About the game

Roadistan is a 3D endless runner set on the busy evening roads of Lahore. Swipe through traffic, jump potholes, dodge cows and wrong-way bikes, pick up passengers and deliver them for fare and tip. Short runs, big fun — made for any phone.

  • 3-lane endless runner — swipe to change lane, jump, and use the horn.
  • 20-zone Lahore tour — drive from zone to zone through famous Lahore places.
  • Orange Line & Metro Bus — spot them on the road as you go.
  • Power-ups — Magnet, Bumper and Double Kiraya.
  • Lifelines — a hit costs a jaan, but you can win one back.
  • Garage and upgrades — unlock new vehicles and upgrade them.
  • English + Roman Urdu — play in your language.
  • Works offline, no ads, free — no internet needed, no accounts.
