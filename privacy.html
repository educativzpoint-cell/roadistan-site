Roadistan — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026 (applies to Roadistan version 1.0)

Roadistan is an offline endless-runner game for Android made by Educativz ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. Short version: we collect nothing.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no login of any kind.
  • We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data.
  • No ads, no advertising ID, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party SDKs that collect data are in version 1.0.
  • The game does not request the internet permission and does not send anything over the network by itself.

What stays on your phone

The game keeps a few files in its own private storage on your device, only so it can work:

  • Save file — best score, totals (runs, distance, passengers), driver rank, your in-game Rupees, vehicles and upgrades, settings (sound, vibration, graphics, language) and tutorial progress.
  • Small technical log — frame rate, loading times and basic phone details (model, graphics chip, memory) recorded for checking the game's performance. It is never sent anywhere.

These files never leave your device. They are not backed up to the cloud by the game, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings.

Permissions

  • Vibrate — a short vibration when you hit something. You can turn it off in the game's Settings.

No other permission is requested.

Sharing and links

  • The Share buttons open WhatsApp (or your web browser, if WhatsApp is not installed) through a wa.me link with a ready-made message: your run's numbers (score, Rupees, best score, driver rank) and a link to the game on Google Play. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it in WhatsApp, and we never receive the message. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies there.
  • The Privacy button opens this page in your browser.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Google Play

Roadistan is delivered through Google Play. Google may process data about installs and crashes under its own privacy policy; we do not control that.

Changes

If ads (or anything else new) are added in a future update, this policy will be updated first, before that update is released, and the date above will change.
